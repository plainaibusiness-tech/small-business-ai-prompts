# Small business AI prompt templates

Free, copy-and-paste prompts for everyday small business paperwork. Fill in the [brackets], paste into the AI tool you already use, and read the result before you send it.

Made by the YouTube channel Plain AI for Business: https://www.youtube.com/@PlainAIforBiz

## What is in here

- `invoices.md`: create an invoice, email it, and send a polite payment reminder
- `quotes.md`: write a professional quote and a short email to send with it

## Rules we follow

1. Read every number out loud and check the total adds up.
2. Never paste a client's bank details or private information into an AI tool.
3. The prompts tell the AI not to add anything you did not give it. Keep it that way.
4. These are writing aids, not legal, tax or accounting advice.

# Customer emails

Fill in the [brackets], paste into ChatGPT, and read the result before you send it. Written for ChatGPT. Not tested on other assistants. Remove real customer names and details from anything you paste in.

## 1. Reply to a common question

I run [type of business]. A customer wrote: "[paste the question, names removed]". Write a short, friendly reply in 3 to 5 sentences. Answer the question directly. If any detail is missing, put [CHECK] there instead of guessing.

## 2. Make replies sound like you

Here are two emails I wrote: [paste 2 old emails, names removed]. Describe my writing style in 5 bullet points. Then write the reply below in that style: [paste the customer message].

## 3. Say no politely

A customer asked for [request]. I can't do it because [reason]. Write a kind reply that says no, gives the reason in one sentence, and offers [alternative]. Keep it under 100 words.

## 4. Follow up after no reply

I sent a customer [what you sent] on [date] and have not heard back. Write a short follow-up that is polite, easy to answer with one word, and does not sound pushy.

## Before you send

- Check names, dates, prices and promises yourself.
- Only promise what you can really do.
- Keep the final say. The AI drafts, you decide.

# Late payment reminders

Three short reminders for an overdue invoice, from first to last. Fill in the [brackets], paste into the AI tool you already use, and read the result before you send it.

## Prompt 1: first reminder

```
Write a short, friendly email reminding a customer that an invoice is past due.
Use only the details below. Do not add fees, dates, threats or promises I did not give you.
Customer: [customer, or a role like the customer]
Invoice number: [number]
Amount: [amount and currency]
Original due date: [date]
How to pay: [payment method I use]
Tone: polite and calm. Under 80 words.
```

## Prompt 2: second reminder

```
Write a clear, polite second reminder for an unpaid invoice.
Use only these facts: customer [customer, or a role like the customer], invoice [number], amount [amount], due date [date], payment method [method].
Mention that I sent a first reminder on [date].
Ask them to reply with a payment date, or tell me if there is a problem with the invoice.
Do not add late fees or legal wording. Under 100 words.
```

## Prompt 3: last reminder

```
Write a firm but respectful last reminder for an unpaid invoice.
Facts: customer [customer, or a role like the customer], invoice [number], amount [amount], due date [date], earlier reminders sent on [dates].
Ask for payment or a call by [date I choose].
Do not threaten, and do not mention any fee or legal step unless I list it here: [only what my own contract says, or leave empty].
Under 120 words.
```

## Check before you send

1. Does the amount, invoice number and date match your own invoice?
2. Did the AI add anything you did not give it? Delete it.
3. Is the tone one you would use with this customer?
4. Never paste bank details or private customer data into an AI tool. Add them yourself afterwards.
5. If you used a role instead of a name, fix the greeting.

These are writing aids, not legal, tax or accounting advice. Late fees and interest depend on your contract and local rules.

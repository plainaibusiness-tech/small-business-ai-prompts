# Complaint reply prompts

Fill in the [brackets], paste into the AI tool you already use, and read the reply before you send it. Never paste customer names, addresses, phone numbers or payment details into an AI prompt.

## 1. Calm first reply

Write a short, polite reply to a customer complaint.

What happened (in my words): [what the customer says went wrong]
What I know is true: [facts you checked]
What I can offer: [refund, redo, replacement, or nothing yet]
My tone: [friendly, formal, short]

Rules: say sorry for the problem without blaming anyone. Do not invent facts, promises or deadlines. Keep it under 120 words.

## 2. Reply when you need more details

Write a short reply asking this customer for the details I need to fix the problem: [what you need, for example order date, photo, what they expected]. Be polite and ask only for what is needed. Do not promise a result yet.

## 3. Reply when you cannot give what they ask

Write a kind, clear reply that says no to this request: [the request]. The reason is: [real reason]. Offer this instead: [real alternative]. Do not sound defensive. Do not invent policies.

## Before you send

Read it out loud. Remove anything that is not true for your business. Check names, dates and amounts yourself.

# Invoice prompts

## 1. Create the invoice

You are my invoicing assistant. Create a professional invoice from these facts. Business name: [your business]. Client: [client name]. Invoice number: [next in your sequence]. Items: [each piece of work with its price]. Payment details: [how you want to be paid]. Terms: due in 7 days. Format it as clean invoice text I can paste into a document, with an itemized table, a subtotal, and a clear total. Do not add anything I did not give you.

## 2. Email to send with it

Turn this invoice into a short, friendly email I can send with it. Mention the total and the due date, and thank them for the work.

## 3. Payment reminder

Write a polite payment reminder for this invoice. Friendly but firm. One line about the late fee only if my terms include one.

## The 7 things every invoice needs

1. Your business name and contact details
2. The client's name
3. A unique invoice number
4. The date it was sent
5. An itemized list of what they are paying for
6. The total, big and clear
7. How to pay, and by when

# Quote prompts

## 1. Write the quote

Write a professional quote for my business. I run a [type of business]. The job is: [describe the work]. Include a short intro, a clear scope of work, an itemized price list that adds up to [total], an estimated timeline, and these terms: quote valid for 30 days, 50% deposit to start, balance on completion. Keep it under 200 words, friendly and confident.

## 2. Email to send with it

Turn this quote into a short, friendly email I can send to the customer. Add one line that creates gentle urgency to reply, and keep the formatting clean for a phone screen.

## The 6 things every quote needs

1. Scope of work
2. What is included and what is not
3. Itemized price
4. Timeline
5. How long the price is valid
6. Payment terms and contact details

# Reply to an online review

Paste this into the AI tool you already use. Fill in the [brackets]. Read the reply before you post it.

## Prompt
You help me reply to a customer review for my small business.
Business: [what you do, city]
Review (paste it): [review text]
Stars: [number]
What actually happened, if I know: [facts]
Next step I want to offer: [e.g. invite them to message us, none yet]

Rules:
- Use only the facts I give you. Do not promise refunds, discounts or fixes I did not approve.
- Replies are public. Never include private details (order numbers, phone numbers, health or money details).
- Keep it under 70 words, warm and direct, no hype.
- If it is a complaint, thank them, say what you understand went wrong, and give one next step.
- If a fact is missing, ask me one question first.
- End with CHECK BEFORE SENDING listing the 2 things I must verify.

## Check before you post
Names and dates are right, nothing promised that you did not approve, no private details about the customer.
